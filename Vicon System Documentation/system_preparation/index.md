# Welcome!

This guide will take you through the steps of setting up the Vicon motion capture system, utilising the Vicon Nexus software (version 2.18) and the Vicon Vero cameras (version 2.2).

To get started, begin with the [quick start](#System%20Preparation%20Quick-Start) guide below. This will take you through the basics of system calibration.

Once you feel comfortable, or when you need to troubleshoot any calibration issues, you can check out the [full guide](#System%20Preparation%20Guide) below that.
